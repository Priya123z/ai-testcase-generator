As a logged-in customer, I want to complete a purchase from my shopping cart so that items are shipped to my address.

Acceptance criteria:
- Cart with at least one item can proceed to checkout
- Shipping address form validates all required fields (name, street, city, postcode, country)
- Order summary shows correct subtotal, shipping cost, and total
- Successful payment redirects to order confirmation page with order number
- Failed payment shows error message and does not empty the cart
- Empty cart prevents navigation to checkout
